How a send travels

  1. The window calls one Convex action with number, name, campaign and parameters.
  2. The action checks the country code, the allowlist and two rate limits, then logs the attempt.
  3. It posts to AiSensy's campaign endpoint with the key held on the deployment. The browser never sees it.
  4. AiSensy's answer is written back to the thread, verbatim, accepted or refused.

Where it could plug in

  • Antenne digest: one message when a story ships on the fleet feed, campaign dispatch-ship.
  • Echeance alerts: a credential expires in seven days, campaign echeance-expiry.
  • Balise reports: a visitor filed a correction, the owner hears about it on the phone.
  • Rappel reminders: cards due today, sent at the hour the learner picked.

Each is one approved template and one API campaign name. The relay does not change.

Numbers typed here are kept in this browser and in the relay log; nothing is shared beyond AiSensy.
